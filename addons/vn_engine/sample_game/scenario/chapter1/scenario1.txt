# start
@chapter_title "Qaan's Tour" "A feature walkthrough" 2.0
@bg bg_dawn with fade 1.0
Narrator: Welcome. This chapter is a short tour of what VN Engine can do, no art or audio files needed. Press F3 to open the debug panel.

# greet
@datecard "Day One" "The Old Observatory"
Qaan: Hi, I'm Qaan. I run this place. Want the full tour, or should we skip ahead?\nPress F3 again to close the debug panel.
- Give me the full tour. -> tour
- Skip ahead. -> skip

# tour
@flow "The Full Tour"
@flag took_tour = true
@set_var warmth = 1
Qaan: A name before the colon picks the speaker, and its color comes from the character file. A bare Narrator: line, like the one before this, shows no name at all. Maybe I will change it in the future to make it narrator or something like that.
@call aside

# after_tour
Qaan: That line came right after the aside. "@return" brings you to the node below the one that called it.
Qaan: Your warmth toward me is {$warmth} right now.
Qaan: Hold on.{w=0.5} That was a [wave]lot[/wave] of talking, wasn't it? Words can [bounce]jump[/bounce], [jitter]shiver[/jitter] or [rainbow]shine[/rainbow] too.
@jump hub

# skip
@flow "Skip Ahead"
Qaan: Straight to the point, I like that.
@jump hub

# aside
Qaan: This aside runs through "@call". Once it finishes, we jump right back to where we left off.
@return

# hub
@flow "Workshop Hub"
@bg bg_workshop with wipe_left 0.8
Qaan: Let's look at two more things before we head out.
- Ask about the telescope. -> topic_telescope once
- Ask about Ely. (is this clickable?) -> topic_ely disabled_if met_ely == false hint "Meet Ely first."
- A bonus: peek at Qaan's notes. -> topic_notes disabled_if warmth < 2 once hint "Needs a little more warmth."
- A secret: the roof hatch. -> topic_roof if warmth >= 3 once
- Move on. -> chapter_end
- Bonus: show me the text effects. -> topic_text once

# topic_telescope
@flag warmth += 1
Qaan: The telescope upstairs is older than the observatory itself. That was "@flag", a typed write with clamping and a default from config/flags/.
@flag met_ely = true
@jump hub

# topic_ely
@flag warmth += 1
Ely: Hello. I catalog the stars here. Asking about me just unlocked through "disabled_if", it needed "met_ely" to be true first.
@jump hub

# topic_roof
@flag warmth += 1
Qaan: Nobody knows about the roof hatch. This choice was hidden until warmth reached 3, that's "if" at work. A hidden choice does not show up at all.
@jump hub

# topic_text
@flow "Text Effects"
Qaan: Text effects are BBCode tags around words. Godot has some built in: [wave]wave[/wave], [shake]shake[/shake], [tornado]tornado[/tornado], [rainbow]rainbow[/rainbow] and [pulse]pulse[/pulse].
Qaan: These play once as the letters type: [pop]pop[/pop], [bounce]bounce[/bounce], [drop]drop[/drop], [grow]grow[/grow], [spin]spin[/spin] and [decode]decode[/decode].
Qaan: These keep going: [jitter]jitter[/jitter], [glitch]glitch[/glitch], [ghost]ghost[/ghost], [heartbeat]heartbeat[/heartbeat] and [sway]sway[/sway].
Qaan: Most take options too, like [jitter amp=4]a stronger jitter[/jitter] or a [pop time=0.8 rise=20]slow, high pop[/pop].
@textfx glitch
Qaan: And "@textfx glitch" breaks the whole line, like a bad radio signal, until "@textfx off".
@textfx off
Qaan: Turn on Reduce motion in Settings and the moving ones stand still. The colors stay.
@jump hub

# topic_notes
@flow "Qaan's Notes"
@flag warmth += 1
Qaan: These are just old observation logs, nothing new. This choice was not clickable until warmth reached 2, that's "disabled_if" at work. Now it is clickable.
@jump chapter_end

# chapter_end
@flow "Chapter One End"
@if warmth >= 3
Narrator: Qaan waves warmly. You've now seen speaker colors, choices, @flag, @set_var, and @call/@return.
@elif warmth >= 1
Narrator: Qaan gives a friendly nod. You've seen the basics: choices, flags, and a call/return aside.
@else
Narrator: Qaan shrugs. A quick visit, a quick tour. Chapter two is next either way.
@endif
